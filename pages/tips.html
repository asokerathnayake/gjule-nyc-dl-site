Top 10 Tips for Passing Your NYS Road Test

Passing your road test in New York State can be challenging, but with the right preparation, you can succeed. Here are our top 10 tips: 1. Practice regularly in varied conditions, including different times of day and weather. 2. Master parallel parking, as it's a common failure point. 3. Ensure your vehicle is in good working order with all required documents. 4. Know the pre-test vehicle checks. 5. Stay calm and follow the examiner's instructions precisely.

DMV Checklist: What to Bring on Test Day

Being prepared for test day means having all the necessary documents and ensuring your vehicle is ready. Don't get turned away! Here's what you need: Valid NYS learner permit, original Pre-Licensing Course Certificate (MV-278) or Driver Education Certificate (MV-285), properly registered and insured vehicle, and a licensed driver (age 21+) to accompany you.

Understanding NYS Traffic Point System

Accumulating too many points on your NYS driving record can lead to license suspension. Learn how the point system works, the points for common violations (e.g., speeding, red light), and the Driver Violation Point System (DVPS) consequences. Staying informed helps you maintain a clean record.
